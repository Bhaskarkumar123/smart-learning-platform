Basic Arithmetic

Addition Sum = First number + Second number
Subtraction Difference = Minuend − Subtrahend
Multiplication Product = Multiplicand × Multiplier
Division Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder

Factors and Multiples

Even Number A number divisible by 2
Multiple Number × Whole number
Divisibility by 10 The number ends in 0
Divisibility by 5 The number ends in 0 or 5

Fractions

Equivalent Fractions a/b = (a × k)/(b × k), where b ≠ 0 and k ≠ 0
Fraction of a Quantity Fraction × Quantity
Add Like Fractions a/b + c/b = (a + c)/b
Subtract Like Fractions a/b − c/b = (a − c)/b

Geometry and Measurement

Perimeter of a Rectangle P = 2 × (Length + Breadth)
Area of a Rectangle A = Length × Breadth
Perimeter of a Square P = 4 × Side
Area of a Square A = Side × Side

Decimals and Percentage

Decimal to Percentage Decimal × 100%
Percentage (Part ÷ Whole) × 100%
Decimal Addition Align decimal points before adding.
Decimal Subtraction Align decimal points before subtracting.

Average, Speed and Time

Average Sum of observations ÷ Number of observations
Speed Distance ÷ Time
Distance Speed × Time
Time Distance ÷ Speed
Quick Revision Tip: Read each formula carefully and practise using it with example numbers. Remember to use consistent units in measurement problems.
